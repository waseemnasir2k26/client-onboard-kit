Wholesale ordering portal
Perihelion Studio Ltd and Marlowe & Finch Roasters Ltd
Issued 01 September 2026

This document is the operating manual for the engagement. The Service Agreement says what happens if things go wrong; this says how we work so that they do not. If something here conflicts with the Service Agreement, the Service Agreement wins.

1. Agreed scope

Design and build a self-service ordering portal for Marlowe & Finch wholesale accounts, replacing the current email-and-spreadsheet process, and migrate the existing 60 accounts onto it.

DeliverableWhat it includesTarget date
Discovery and information architectureAccount model, order states, pricing tiers, and a clickable flow map.25 September 2026
Portal buildAuthenticated account area, standing-order templates, order history, CSV export, and an admin view for the Marlowe & Finch team.03 November 2026
Migration and handoverImport of the 60 existing wholesale accounts, one training session, and a written runbook.20 November 2026

Not included, and quoted separately if wanted:

  • Native iOS or Android applications.
  • Integration with the Marlowe & Finch accounting system.
  • Copywriting, photography, or product data entry.
  • Ongoing hosting, monitoring, or support after handover.
  • Search engine optimisation work of any kind.

Use delegated access with a named account wherever the platform supports it. Do not send passwords in chat or email; use a password manager share or a one-time secret link.

6. What "done" looks like

  • 80% of wholesale orders placed through the portal within 60 days of launch.
  • Order-entry admin time reduced from roughly 6 hours a week to under 1.
  • No manual re-keying of orders into the fulfilment sheet.

Hard deadlines

  • Portal live for wholesale accounts - 20 November 2026. Christmas wholesale ordering opens on 24 November.
